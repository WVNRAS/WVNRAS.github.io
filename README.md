# New River Alert System (NRAS)

**Official Website:** https://wvnras.github.io/

## About NRAS

The New River Alert System (NRAS) is a community-focused weather alert project designed to help improve awareness of severe weather and emergency conditions in southern West Virginia.

NRAS uses publicly available National Weather Service (NWS) alert information and explores alternative communication technologies, including Meshtastic, to help distribute important weather information.

## Website Features

- Live National Weather Service alerts
- Information about the NRAS project and its mission
- Examples of weather alerts distributed through Meshtastic
- Educational video demonstrating the system
- Links to NRAS resources

## Important Notice

NRAS is an independent project and is not an official National Weather Service or government emergency alert service.

For official warnings and emergency instructions, consult the National Weather Service and local emergency management authorities.
